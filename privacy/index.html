Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Battery Sync shows the battery levels of your iPhone, iPad and Mac in one place. This policy explains what the app stores, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. It applies to the Battery Sync app, its widgets and its notification extension on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Stored in your private iCloud No tracking

In short

Your data stays in your iCloud

Battery readings are stored in your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot see them.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

Battery Sync uses your Apple Account through iCloud and nothing else.

No location, contacts or serial numbers

The app only reads what it needs to show a battery level.

Delete it any time

Remove a device’s history, or all Battery Sync data, from the app’s Settings.

Data stored in your iCloud

Battery level and charging state

The percentage and whether the device is charging, on battery or full.

Device name and type

The device’s name, whether it is an iPhone, iPad or Mac, and an identifier the app creates for each installation.

Timestamps

When each reading was measured and synced.

Reading context

What triggered each reading, for example the app, a widget refresh or a requested check.

Requests for a fresh reading

When you ask an iPhone or iPad to report, the request, its time and the installation identifiers of the two devices involved.

This private CloudKit database belongs to you and is managed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. Only devices signed in to the same Apple Account can read it. The developer of Battery Sync has no access to it.

Data kept only on your device

Diagnostic log

A record of readings and sync attempts, including app and OS versions, whether the app was in the foreground or background, Low Power Mode, Background App Refresh status and sync errors. It is kept for 10 days and never leaves the device.

Settings

Device names, pinned devices, low-battery alert thresholds and menu bar choices.

Widget data

The latest readings, shared with Battery Sync’s widgets through storage that only Battery Sync can read.

Notifications

Requested battery reports

When one device asks another for a fresh reading, iCloud sends a push to the target through Apple Push Notification service. Pushes are silent by default. If you turn on report notifications, the target shows an alert, reads its battery and updates the alert with the result.

Low-battery alerts

Created on your device from readings already in your iCloud.

Battery Sync does not send your push token to any server of its own.

What Battery Sync does not do

Collect personal details

No location, contacts, photos, hardware serial numbers or advertising identifiers.

Analytics or advertising

No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

Sell or share your data

Your data is never sold or shared, and you are not tracked across apps or websites.

Run its own server

Sync and notifications use Apple’s iCloud and push services only.

Deleting your data

Remove this device and delete history

In Settings. Stops reporting from that device, deletes its readings from your iCloud and clears its local queue and diagnostic log.

Delete account

Deletes Battery Sync data for every device in your iCloud. Your Apple Account itself is not affected.

Pause reporting

Stops new readings from being uploaded without deleting existing ones.

Deleting the app

Removes its local data. Readings already in iCloud stay until you delete them with one of the options above.

More

Children

Battery Sync is a general-audience utility. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the data it stores stays in the user’s own iCloud.

Changes to this policy

If Battery Sync changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/akhil-code/battery-sync-everywhere-pages.
