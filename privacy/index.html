Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Battery Sync shows the battery levels of your iPhone, iPad and Mac in one place. This policy explains what the app stores, where it is stored, who can see it, and how you can delete it. It applies to the Battery Sync app, its widgets and its notification extension on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Stored in your private iCloud No tracking Shared only if you choose

In short

Your data stays in your iCloud

Battery readings are stored in your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot see them.

Shared only with people you choose

Nobody else sees your devices unless you invite them or send them a link. You can remove them at any time.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

Battery Sync uses your Apple Account through iCloud and nothing else.

No location, contacts or serial numbers

The app only reads what it needs to show a battery level.

Delete it any time

Remove a device’s history, or all Battery Sync data, from the app’s Settings.

Data stored in your iCloud

Battery level and charging state

The percentage and whether the device is charging, on battery or full.

Device name and type

The device’s name, whether it is an iPhone, iPad or Mac, and an identifier the app creates for each installation.

Timestamps

When each reading was measured and synced.

Reading context

What triggered each reading, for example the app, a widget refresh or a requested check.

Requests for a fresh reading

When you ask an iPhone or iPad to report, the request, its time and the installation identifiers of the two devices involved.

Sharing details, if you share

The people you invited, as Apple’s sharing screen records them (a name, email address or phone number), whether each has accepted, whether anyone with the link can join, and who may request readings.

Your sharing name

The name people you share with see above your devices, such as “Sam’s devices”. Battery Sync suggests the name on your Apple Account or, on a Mac, your Mac user name. You can change it in Settings → Sharing.

This private CloudKit database belongs to you and is managed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. Only devices signed in to your Apple Account can read it, plus the people you share with, as described below. The developer of Battery Sync has no access to it.

Sharing with other people

How sharing works

Sharing is off until you turn it on in Settings → Sharing. It uses Apple’s iCloud sharing: you invite specific people, or allow anyone with the link to join. Invitations are sent with Apple’s sharing screen, so Battery Sync never reads your contacts.

What people you share with see

The names and types of all your devices, their battery levels, charging state, reading history and times, and your sharing name. They can view this but can’t change or delete anything.

Requesting readings

Off for each person until you turn on Can request readings for them. Then they can ask your iPhones and iPads for a fresh reading. The request is saved in your iCloud with the installation identifier of their device, and iCloud marks which account made it, so your device can limit how often it answers.

Devices shared with you

Another person’s readings stay in their iCloud. Battery Sync on your devices downloads them to show in the app, widgets, Mac menu bar and low-battery alerts, marked with that person’s name. The person sharing can see your name or contact as Apple shows it on the share, and whether you have joined.

Ending sharing

Remove one person, turn off their requests, or stop sharing with everyone in Settings → Sharing. To stop seeing someone else’s devices, use Stop Viewing; their devices are removed from every device on your Apple Account.

Copies already downloaded to someone’s device can stay there until their app next checks iCloud, or until they delete Battery Sync.

Data kept only on your device

Diagnostic log

A record of readings and sync attempts, including app and OS versions, whether the app was in the foreground or background, Low Power Mode, Background App Refresh status and sync errors. It is kept for 10 days and never leaves the device.

Settings

Device names, pinned devices, low-battery alert thresholds, menu bar choices and your sharing name.

Widget data

The latest readings, including devices shared with you, passed to Battery Sync’s widgets through storage that only Battery Sync can read.

Notifications

Requested battery reports

When one device asks another for a fresh reading, iCloud sends a push to the target through Apple Push Notification service. Pushes are silent by default. If you turn on report notifications, the target shows an alert, reads its battery and updates the alert with the result.

Low-battery alerts

Created on your device from readings it has already downloaded, including devices shared with you.

Battery Sync does not send your push token to any server of its own.

What Battery Sync does not do

Collect personal details

No location, contacts, photos, hardware serial numbers or advertising identifiers.

Analytics or advertising

No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

Sell or share your data

Your data is never sold. It is shared only with people you choose, and you are not tracked across apps or websites.

Run its own server

Sync and notifications use Apple’s iCloud and push services only.

Deleting your data

Remove this device and delete history

In Settings. Stops reporting from that device, deletes its readings from your iCloud, so people you share with no longer see it, and clears its local queue and diagnostic log.

Delete account

Deletes Battery Sync data for every device in your iCloud and ends any sharing. Your Apple Account itself is not affected.

Pause reporting

Stops new readings from being uploaded without deleting existing ones.

Deleting the app

Removes its local data. Readings already in iCloud stay until you delete them with one of the options above.

More

Children

Battery Sync is a general-audience utility. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the data it stores stays in the user’s own iCloud.

Changes to this policy

If Battery Sync changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
